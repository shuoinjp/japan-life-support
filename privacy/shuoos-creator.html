PRIVACY POLICY

ShuoOS Creator Factory

Effective date: August 10, 2026

Scope

This policy applies to ShuoOS Creator Factory, a desktop workflow that helps the account owner prepare, approve, upload, and verify promotional media on services such as TikTok.

Information used

When you connect TikTok, the app may receive your TikTok account identifier, display name, username, granted permissions, access token, refresh token, and token expiry information. The app also processes the videos, captions, campaign identifiers, and publication status needed for the publishing action you request.

How information is used

Connected-account information is used only to authenticate your requested action, upload approved content, check processing status, prevent duplicate submissions, and verify the resulting publication. We do not sell this information, use it for advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored locally using macOS Keychain or an equivalent protected local credential store. Campaign records and publication receipts are stored locally for reliability, audit, and duplicate-prevention purposes. Credentials are not written into promotional media or public campaign pages.

Sharing with TikTok

Only after the account owner authorizes a publication does the app transmit the selected video, caption, publication settings, and OAuth authorization to TikTok. TikTok processes that information under TikTok's own terms and privacy policy.

Retention, disconnection, and deletion

You can disconnect the TikTok integration or revoke access from TikTok account settings. To request deletion of locally retained OAuth credentials and related publication records, contact shuoinjp@gmail.com. We will complete a verified deletion request within 30 days, except for records we must retain to meet legal or security obligations.

Children

ShuoOS Creator Factory is intended for adult account owners and is not directed to children.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. For privacy questions, access requests, or deletion requests, contact shuoinjp@gmail.com.
